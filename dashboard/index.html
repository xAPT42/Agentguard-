Risk by dimension
Fleet totals, in scorer points
Dimensions decompose the score emitted by agentguard.risk.scorer. Exposure is the no-authentication penalty (max 40). Agency sums mutating, shell and tool-sprawl signals (max 55). Data Quality is the orphaned/unknown status penalty (max 20). OWASP covers credential exposure and read access (max 14). The four sum to the raw score, which the scorer then caps at 100.
